// Iron nails
// Could be in various sizes of nails
// Recommend consuming nails by weight
// It is one of the simplest projects for any smith
// Smith says you get good at making nails around 
// your 1,000th nail.
// For # nails per pound by length see
// https://www.treeisland.com/sites/default/files/documents/brochures-spec-sheets/Average%20nails%20per%20pound.pdf
// Iron nails at top of menu as when you need them 
// you tend need to make lots
// Iron nail recipe also counts for: rivet, pivots and other
// small iron simple shaped pieces

.Iron nails. "Hunting Horn" [effort:4] [phys:arms,hands,stance] *CARPENTRY* %10% /2h/ |-1| [assist:1]
{*iron billet} #1# [ground] [remove] '+to make nails'
{*forge*}			 [noquality] [ground]	'+as a forge or furnace'
{Charcoal} 	(6) 	[remove] [noquality] [ground]
{Bellows} 			 [ground]
{*anvil*}			 [ground]		'+as an anvil nearby'
{*hammer}						'+as any hammer'
[MATERIAL:iron]
[WEIGHT:1]
[PRICE: 4]
[TILEGFX:bc-billet]
//price simple worked iron at 4 x weight
