Note, for a recipe like below, the in-game desciption of the needed item will show in full:
{*hammer}					<- ☐ *hammer
{*hammer} 'Hammer'			<- ☐ Hammer
{*hammer} '+Hammer'			<- ☐ *hammer Hammer
{Hammer} '+for hammering'	<- ☐ Hammer for hammering
{*hammer} '+for hammering'	<- ☐ *hammer for hammering

-> thus it is very important to rewrite a complete sentences for item that use a wildcard.

Other examples:

{*birch-bark strip}	#0.5#	[remove]					<-	☐ 0.5 lbs of *birch-bark strip
{*birch-bark strip}	#0.5#	[remove] 'Birch-bark strip' <- 	☐ 0.5 lbs of birch-bark strip
Note that 'Birch' gets de-capitalized in the 2nd example
